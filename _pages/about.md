<div>
    I am a PhD student at the University of Toronto, working with <a href="https://janders.eecg.utoronto.ca/">Professor Jason Anderson</a>. My research centers on the architecture of Coarse-Grained Reconfigurable Arrays (CGRAs) — a class of reconfigurable architectures capable of delivering near-ASIC performance and energy efficiency while retaining runtime programmability. I work on designing and exploring CGRA architectures, and also develop CAD mapping algorithms to efficiently execute applications on these devices. I also completed a research internship at <a href="https://www.r-ccs.riken.jp/en/">RIKEN Center for Computational Science (R-CCS)</a> with Dr. Kentaro Sano.
    <br><br>
    Before starting my PhD, I worked as a Silicon Design Engineer at AMD in India. During my undergraduate studies in Electronics, I interned with Prof. Paolo Ienne at EPFL on the <a href="https://dynamatic.epfl.ch/">Dynamatic</a> project, with Prof. Kamakoti at IIT Madras on the <a href="https://shakti.org.in/">Shakti</a> processor project, and participated in Google Summer of Code with BeagleBoard.org for the <a href="https://beaglewire.github.io/">BeagleWire</a> project.
    <br><br>
    I am always open to research collaborations and opportunities in Computer Architecture, Reconfigurable Computing, and RTL Design &amp; Verification. Feel free to reach out if you'd like to discuss ideas or any of my previous work.
